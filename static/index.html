How this experimental estimate works

The estimate uses at least three independent reference sites within 25 miles, with readings no older than 30 minutes and no more than 15 minutes apart. Up to eight nearby sites contribute. Temperature and dewpoint use a distance-weighted median to limit the influence of a single unusual reading. Wind speed is averaged; direction uses wind vectors and the nearest of eight compass directions. Co-located sites count once. Community stations remain visible for comparison but do not affect the estimate. Temperature, dewpoint and wind each need three usable reports. Wind exposure and sensor heights vary between networks.

This is an observation-based estimate, not a validated analysis or an exact measurement at your location. There is no model gap filling, time extrapolation or smoothing of actual weather changes. Station spread is not an accuracy guarantee. Cloud cover, rain and other present-weather descriptions are not inferred from temperature. The collector checks public feeds every two minutes; delivery delays remain visible.

About this radar

Native Level II lowest-tilt surveillance reflectivity is collected every minute for KMPX. Completed low-level scans are published from the real-time chunk feed without waiting for the full radar volume; supplemental low-level scans are included when produced. KMPX uses its 0.5° base tilt. Scan frequency is controlled by the radar operator and is not guaranteed. Incomplete sweeps are withheld, and the completed-volume archive fills the previous two hours. Every available completed scan within that rolling two-hour window is included, with no fixed frame-count limit. Super-resolution scans have 250-meter range gates and 0.5° azimuth spacing; the beam footprint widens with distance. Display pixels sample the nearest native gate without smoothing. The beam rises with distance and does not directly measure rainfall at the surface. Times are shown in Central time.

Radar: NOAA/NWS Level II data via NSF Unidata public archive and real-time feed. Map boundaries: Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Faint, label-free major roads: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb.
