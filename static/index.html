About these observations

Routine official airport METARs from NOAA Aviation Weather Center, including ASOS and AWOS reports. The map updates once an hour at :02, allowing a short arrival buffer. Each hourly snapshot accepts only reports timestamped :50–:59 of the preceding hour; the report nearest :53 is preferred. For example, 7:53 PM is labeled 8 PM, and its actual report time remains visible in the popup. SPECI reports and rapid MADISHF reports are excluded. Stations reporting outside this window are omitted. Twelve real hourly snapshots are backfilled automatically during setup.

Temperature and dew point are in °F; wind speed is mph. Missing or physically implausible values are removed, and dew point cannot exceed temperature beyond a small rounding tolerance. These are basic range and time checks, not complete instrument quality assurance. Missing reports stay missing.

Observations: NOAA Aviation Weather Center. State and county boundary tiles: Iowa Environmental Mesonet.

About this radar

Native Level II lowest-tilt surveillance reflectivity is collected every minute for KMPX. Completed low-level scans are published from the real-time chunk feed without waiting for the full radar volume; supplemental low-level scans are included when produced. KMPX uses its 0.5° base tilt. Scan frequency is controlled by the radar operator and is not guaranteed. Incomplete sweeps are withheld, and the completed-volume archive fills the previous two hours. Every available completed scan within that rolling two-hour window is included, with no fixed frame-count limit. Super-resolution scans have 250-meter range gates and 0.5° azimuth spacing; the beam footprint widens with distance. Display pixels sample the nearest native gate without smoothing. The beam rises with distance and does not directly measure rainfall at the surface. Times are shown in Central time.

Radar: NOAA/NWS Level II data via NSF Unidata public archive and real-time feed. Map boundaries: Iowa Environmental Mesonet.
