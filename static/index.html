Sources and timing

Only official routine airport METARs from NOAA Aviation Weather Center are used. One report per airport is selected in each :45–:59 window, closest to :53, and labeled with the following hour. For example, an 8:53 p.m. report appears in the 9 p.m. frame. SPECI and high-frequency MADIS reports, road stations and mesonets are not included. Collection starts at :00 and rechecks every two minutes through :20 so late batches can fill in. If the entire hour is still missing, retries continue every two minutes. Once the catch-up window ends and reports are available, later checks reuse the saved hour. Page refreshes only read saved data. The preceding two hours are revisited at each scheduled collection to recover late deliveries. While the newest hour has no reports, the map follows the latest available hour and labels that actual hour; it advances automatically as new reports arrive. Reports arriving after the catch-up window can be recovered by the next hour’s overlap collection. Missing station-hours are left missing, with no earlier report carried forward.

All eligible airports in the existing Upper Midwest extent (40–50°N, 100–85°W) are included. KCFE, KFCE, KSGS and KDEH are no longer excluded. KFCM, KMIC and KMSP are plotted individually. All airport reports are retained. Overlapping plots are temporarily hidden with a small gap; spacing stays consistent across the twelve hourly frames. Zooming in reveals nearby airports. The list includes all reporting airports for the selected hour, including those outside the current map view. Missing or invalid measurements show as unknown while the rest of the station plot remains available.

The Sun angle is current for Hopkins and does not follow the history slider. Cloud heights are available on the separate Clouds tab.

Reports: NOAA Aviation Weather Center. Sun: NOAA calculation details.

About this radar

Native Level II lowest-tilt surveillance reflectivity is collected every minute for KMPX. Completed low-level scans are published from the real-time chunk feed without waiting for the full radar volume; supplemental low-level scans are included when produced. KMPX uses its 0.5° base tilt. Scan frequency is controlled by the radar operator and is not guaranteed. Incomplete sweeps are withheld, and the completed-volume archive fills the previous two hours. Every available completed scan within that rolling two-hour window is included, with no fixed frame-count limit. Super-resolution scans have 250-meter range gates and 0.5° azimuth spacing; the beam footprint widens with distance. Display pixels sample the nearest native gate without smoothing. The beam rises with distance and does not directly measure rainfall at the surface. Times are shown in Central time.

Radar: NOAA/NWS Level II data via NSF Unidata public archive and real-time feed. Map boundaries: Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Faint, label-free major roads: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb.
